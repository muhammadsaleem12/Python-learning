## Game Character Stats Tracker

Completed another small Python OOP practice project: **Game Character Stats Tracker**.

The project simulates a basic game character with a name, health, mana, and level. I used properties and setters to make sure health stays between `0` and `100` and mana stays between `0` and `50`.

I also added a `level_up()` method that increases the character's level and restores their health and mana to their maximum values.

This project gave me more practice with **properties, getters, setters, encapsulation, methods, object state, and `__str__`**.

The main thing I wanted to practice here was controlling an object's data through setters instead of allowing values to change without any rules.

It's a small project, but it was good practice for building classes that actually manage their own state.
