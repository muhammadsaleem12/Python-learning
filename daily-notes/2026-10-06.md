Today I worked on the **Polygon Area Calculator** project in Python.

The project uses `Rectangle` and `Square` classes to perform different geometric calculations and demonstrate Object-Oriented Programming concepts.

### Key Takeaway

The biggest concept I practiced today was **inheritance**.

The `Square` class inherits from `Rectangle`, which allows it to reuse existing functionality while changing certain behaviors to follow the rules of a square.

I also practiced thinking about objects as things that contain both **data and behavior**. A rectangle has dimensions, but it can also calculate its area, perimeter, diagonal, picture, and how many other shapes can fit inside it.
